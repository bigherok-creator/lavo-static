Corporate Overview

A Legacy of Clinical Beauty

For over 15 years, LAVO has operated as the highly secured backend manufacturing powerhouse for thousands of clinical salons and premium aesthetic brands across the globe.

Architects of the Modern Market

Established in 2009, LAVO recognized a critical market gap: the lack of true clinical efficacy in commercial cosmetic manufacturing. We scaled our operations specifically to service clinical, professional, and dermatological demands.

We are not simply a co-packer. We are chemical engineers, regulatory scientists, and supply chain architects. We take raw biological briefs and engineer them into market-dominating retail units, completely invisible to the end consumer.
