Biotechnological Research Vectors

We specialize in advanced lipid complexes, cell-signaling peptides, and microbiome-friendly systems designed specifically for high-efficacy beauty sectors.

Biomimetic Peptides & DNA

Synthesizing hair-identical protein structures for deep cortex repair without relying on superficial silicons. Includes PDRN (Polydeoxyribonucleotide) applications.

Liposomal Encapsulation

Protecting volatile actives (like Retinol or Vitamin C) in phospholipid bilayers to safely bypass the stratum corneum and release directly at target cell sites.

Fermentation & Post-Biotics

Utilizing lactobacillus fermentation filtrates to drastically reduce compound molecular weight, improving trans-epidermal penetration and scalp biome equilibrium.

Advanced Surfactant Physics

Developing high-foaming, low-irritation cleansing matrices using sulfate-free amino acid co-surfactants engineered to prevent structural hair-swelling.

Low-pH Oxidative Dyes

Vibrant, fade-resistant color deposition technology formulated in alkaline-free environments to preserve the scalp acid mantle.

Clean Beauty & Silicone-Alts

Developing high-slip, heat-protecting platforms utilizing plant-derived alkanes to replace cyclic silicones (D4, D5) per EU regulations.

QA/QC Formulation Validation Gates

No formula enters mass production without clearing our 12-week internal testing protocols. We do not compromise on product safety or stability.

Gate 01

Thermal Stability Cycling

Continuous cycling between -10°C and 45°C to assess phase separation and emulsion structural collapse over a simulated 2-year shelf life.

Gate 02

PET (Preservative Efficacy Testing)

Rigorous microbial challenge testing (ISO 11930) inoculating formulas with Pseudomonas and Aspergillus to guarantee broad-spectrum defense.

Gate 03

Rheological & pH Drift Analysis

Utilizing Brookfield viscometers to map yield stress and flow behavior, ensuring viscosity holds alongside zero pH drifting.

Gate 04

Packaging Compatibility

Testing plasticizers and bulk/package migration (leaching) inside ovens to prevent container swelling, paneling, or formula oxidation.
